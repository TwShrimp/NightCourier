Before adding an external free asset, record its author, exact source URL, license/version, download date, modifications, and required attribution here. Keep the supplied license alongside the asset when redistribution permits it. Verify that its terms allow including the source files in a public GitHub repository; a zero price alone does not establish that permission.

The scenic bypass centreline, road and terrain meshes, bridge piers, water material, road signs, and minimap texture are also authored/generated by project code. No external pack was added for the road expansion.

The second city, intercity expressway, toll plazas, noise barriers, depot, garage, civic facilities, EV chargers, battery UI, and eight parcel variants are also project-authored procedural geometry and code. They introduce no downloaded asset license.
